When you've spent a professional eternity or two in an environment where
you shape your thoughts into the molds of the surrounding opinions and sensitivities, you lose touch with your voice.
_Now to clarify I do not mean the kind of sensitivities right wing bros might complain about,
I mean the more topical kind running in abundance in company cultures blocking honest ideation outside a selected group._

For me the best way to speak with my voice is to have clarity in what it is trying to say, tease out between the impulses,
moderators and internal redirects what I actually think and is in line with my values.
I love doing this as a team sport in open, safe conversation, but Writing is the great solo activity to get there.
