Of course, as somebody who has gotten to lead a lot of people, having the doing taken out of my hands is nothing new to me. There are many different levels of "doing" to be found in giving direction and guidance. Doing that kind of "doing" excellently also requires thought and clarity. Putting thinking and doing into that clarity has served me well, and, more importantly, the people I'm responsible for. That's why I'm seeking out those new levels of doing, and the clarity they demand.
