If controlled right, coding agents are a great [uplift in productivity for the skilled and an amazing enabler for the less experienced](/post/skill-gap-induced-ai-awe).
But what should we do with this gain?

How should we spend the agentic development dividend?

In this short piece I would like to make the argument that we should use it to ease user experience.
Developers always
The brilliant developer and experience designer [Larry Tesler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Tesler) encapsulated
that tension in his [Law of the conservation of complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_conservation_of_complexity).
>"...every application has an inherent amount of complexity that cannot be removed or hidden. Instead, it must be dealt with, either in product development or in user interaction."
